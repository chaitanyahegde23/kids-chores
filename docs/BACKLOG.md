# Kids Chores engagement backlog

This backlog turns the kid-engagement ideas into independently deliverable outcomes. Priorities favor autonomy, visible personal progress, useful routines, and financial learning over competitive or chance-based mechanics.

## Product guardrails

- Do not add sibling leaderboards or public comparison.
- Do not add loot boxes, random rewards, or manipulative scarcity.
- Keep currencies understandable; new balances must have immutable ledgers.
- Do not erase long-term progress after one missed day.
- Parent-controlled deductions require a description and remain auditable.
