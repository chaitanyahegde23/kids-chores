# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, focus on the work, respect privacy, and use synthetic data when discussing household or child-related scenarios.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or disruptive. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
